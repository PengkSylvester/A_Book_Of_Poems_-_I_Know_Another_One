\marginnote[1mm]{
    \textit{Life, liberty, and the pursuit of relief from uncertainty. Because happiness emerges for me as a byproduct of when uncertainty briefly disappears. Such as when . Excitement and anxiety are the two ends of the spectrum for me in a sine wave of feelings regarding the predictability of the future.}}

``Money can't buy happiness'' I guess they say in the West

Yet they bought and sold my slave ancestor named Happiness

``You will be addressed in the way you are dressed'',

Yet ``Don't judge a book by its cover'' is also stressed.

Make it make sense, that some say ``Dress to impress'',

While yet some say ``Nobody cares about your Rolex''

I say ``Follow your heart, advices may contradict, do your best

Choose your own path, and that'll make all the difference.''
