\marginnote[1mm]{\textit{One must stay drunk on writing so reality cannot destroy you.} \newthought{- Ray Bradbury}}

I sit and dream beneath a team of trees with gleams of golden hair,
I see some birds clothed with brown and red, boldly floating in the air
They flit and dart, they tweet and fart, the sweet beats they emanate
The soft tunes of their whistling keeps me kept in a hypnotized state.
They seem to sing, “Come fly with us.\ You have the feather of a geese”
But they don't know that it's for show as a quill pen for writing these
For my recommended gem of a friend whom I can't believe I'd tried to prevent
I sit and rhyme at 6 A.M.\ dreaming of informing them it's for Nkem
