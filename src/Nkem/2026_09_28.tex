\marginnote[1mm]{\textit{Prioritize consistency over intensity in work and exercise.}}

Day 9 of fasting.\ This was the day I broke my streak.

I prayed for strength and ate creamed custard which was so nostalgic.

Then I went out and just experienced my first ever sugar crash

It was a sickly weakly bodily feeling that felt kinda harsh

I felt nauseous and light-headed, and so freaking tired

My body was done digesting the creamed custard and fruit salad

Next time when I have work I want to accomplish and to finish

I'd better demolish a bunch of carbs, maybe some fries and fish.

I tried to power through it powerlessly, in the end I was beat

Too many sweets after a fast.\ I'd better watch what I eat
