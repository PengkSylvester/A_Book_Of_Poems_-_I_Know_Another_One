The marshmallow test for tots is thought to test who has patience the best


The plan is that if the toddler waits, they double the treat that they left


The readiness to delay the feast is seen as a marker for life success

The kids who eat their treat in haste, are seen as high-chanced for theft




The scientists didn't ask whether it's a correlation or causation by influence

The science assumes that the tested kids all come from equal levels of abundance


The choices chosen by the children may be because they have rich or poor parents


The socioeconomic background of each child made some hungrier than the rest
