    \textit{Building a project in the dark without letting people see it early on is the best way to make something nobody wants.}}

Dear Pastor, I hope this letter finds you feeling supreme.
I seek to acquaint you to my affectionate esteem for Nkem.
Your daughter sir, who I do not have any bad intentions for.
She's a person of interest to me and I would like to know her more,
If I may prove myself to be worthy of that honour, I pray,
To get your blessing, so I know you know I do not come to play.
My dear, I dare to request the favour of an evening conversation,
to fully explain myself to you for your positive satisfaction.
If you decline, I still remain Yours Faithfully, sir. \textit{Signed}, Engr.~Pink Silver
