\marginnote[1mm]{\textit{A great deal of talent is lost to the world for want of a little courage}
\newthought{- Sydney Smith}}

When I was younger, my Dad used to complain that I ate too much

Now I'm skipping meals for fun when I can buy myself any lunch

I used to pack in so many calories, now the pendulum has swung

I think I'll fast for at least 10 days, and maybe I'll go on

I am still fully functional and not hungry.\ May God be with me.

My dream is to someday successfully make it up to day 40.

All I need is my waters with these electrolyte per diem,

4 grams sodium, 4 grams potassium, 400 milligrams magnesium
