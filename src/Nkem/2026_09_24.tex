When I was younger, my Dad used to complain that I ate too much

Now I'm skipping meals for fun when I can buy myself any lunch

I used to pack in so many calories, I should no be a fat guy

cereal or tea

Now I'm not so interested in spoon after spoon of jedi-jedi

The pendulum has swung. The Pengk you knew has sworn

I'll fast myself for at least 10 days, and maybe I'll go on

All I need is my water with these electrolyte per diem,

4g sodium, 4g potassium, 400mg magnesium
