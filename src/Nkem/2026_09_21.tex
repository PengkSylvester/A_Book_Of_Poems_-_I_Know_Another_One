\marginnote[1mm]{\textit{The only time one should peer into another's bowl is to make sure that they have enough.}}

As you go about life you must suffer some pain.

Here's a virtue that averts you from going insane.

Feelings are just things, neither good nor bad for you.
Throw a tantrum or think of it as ``oh look, something new''.

Catch yourself feel those feelings felt true and through.


Your body wants change, or food, or a task it wants to do.

Take some time to breathe or pray on each new feeling, each new day.

Patience is a virtue, one not to be thrown away.
