Here's a poem for Nkem titled `You're the vibe' or `The feeling of you'
I like your energy, your authentic chill aura, your fire burns true and through.


We get along quite well, as kindred spirits, I ship us as \textsc{pinkem}
I yearn to meet you soon enough, and to make you feel lovely to no end.


I plan to write you every morning, or at least every day,
May be on different topics, whatever musings come my way to say.

So this is the third of my many letters to keep up with our compatibility,
To see if you do not, \emph{or} \textit{do} enjoy these doses of vit. me.

I must bid you good evening now so I can rest well for tomorrow.\ Adieu.
Take care of your self as you know how to do boo, sweet dreams, God bless you
