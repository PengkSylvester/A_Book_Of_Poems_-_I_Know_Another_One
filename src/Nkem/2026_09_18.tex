\marginnote[1mm]{\textit{A good word costs no more than a bad one.}}

Why do young men these days wear neckties?
It serves no purpose in my eyes.
It doesn't give warmth like a scarf.
It doesn't fasten shirts like a button.
Some old white men once wore it to stand out,
and now, decades later, it's the standard?
It's an unnatural unfunctional addition to me;
a fashion accessory with no Nigerian history.
It's also an uncomfortable item since my neck has some girth.
It isn't effective for a tourniquet either, like a belt.
If I ever need to console someone in grief,
It can't wipe tears like a handkerchief.
I will admit it does save a shirt from drips and dribble
But I don't need a bib at all times like a baby, simple.
In these closing clothing statements, I hope I helped you realise
that the no-more-necktie revolution will not be televised.
