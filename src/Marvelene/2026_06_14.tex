\marginnote[1mm]{
    \textit{Unhappiness is the product of the number of times per minute that I believe circumstances should be otherwise. By contrast, I can, right now, try believing that what’s happening is what’s supposed to be happening, in exactly the right order.} \newthought{- Henrik Karlsson}}

I am perfectly capable of privately spoiling my wonderful marriage at any time,
by noticing that it’s not the relationship I would’ve imagined being in.
I could notice she's bad with money, words, decisions, planning, and so on.

Meanwhile, a few months before meeting her, I had wished for someone to love,
I wrote down a list of characteristics that the perfect partner would have,
Then prayed she would bring herself to me, and I subsequently met Marve.

If she was perfect at everything, my leadership would be wholly unnecessary,
but the human world will always have spectrums, and so the best parts of me
should serve in partnership with the bests of her, for a more competent unity.
