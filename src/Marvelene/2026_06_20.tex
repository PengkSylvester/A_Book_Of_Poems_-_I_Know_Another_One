\marginnote[1mm]{\textit{Does returning empties (which may not be bone dry) count as transporting open liquor bottles?}}

Uwaoma Papa P, I'm so sorry about last night's missed calls fiasco
It wasn't cool of me to have left the house without a phone.
I had thought we were going for a simple picking up of drinks.
It wasn't until we got there that we met the Dar Es Salaam Police
Who started asking us for a bunch of paperwork as if we were traffickers
Who were trying to smuggle the whiskeys and champagnes across borders.
I'm glad we were able to leave with all the boxes in the end,
But it was not cool that it cost a bribery of 3,100 Rand.
I believe that I fucked up and I'm not usually that dull.
I apologise Pops.\ I should have done better to protect your alcohol.
