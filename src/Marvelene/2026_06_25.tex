When tech shows up, it offers a shortcut, convenience, more degrees of freedom.

I can use Google Maps to navigate anywhere without paying much attention.

I can use AI to write a proposal and get an average draft the first try.

Amazon, Tesla, Samsung, are all pretty much trying to save my time.

And my brain has started to see these speeds as the default for productivity.

So I propose that once in a while, I should try to survive without the utility.

Reintroduce friction to my work life, and ditch the modern for mediocre ways.

To simply remember what my powers are, and to hone those skills for darker days.
