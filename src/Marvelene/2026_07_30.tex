\marginnote[1mm]{\textit{
Almost every successful person begins with two beliefs.
The future can be better than the present, and I have the power to make it so.} \newthought{- David Brooks}}


Since the dawn of civilization, females and males in every society


Have commonly made stable, mated relationships to start or merge a family


But what's the purpose, what's the utility of the elaborate ceremony?


Well, according to Stephanie Coontz's 2005 book `Marriage, a History'


The most important function of wedding was not to procreate progeny


But to secure the adoption of parents, in-laws, beneficial elderly


The newlyweds did the dance to establish secure ties of solidarity


To get important connections in community for their own personal prosperity.
