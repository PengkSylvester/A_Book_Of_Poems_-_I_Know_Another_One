\marginnote[1mm]{\textit{Gameplay is taking on unnecessary obstacles voluntarily to create the struggle to overcome them.} \newthought{- Bernard Suits}}

I play too much and you play too little
My skin is tough and yours is brittle
You're always wanting to hang out later
I wish there's a game we play together
Chess, football, clash, or what have you
An indirect hang-out in something to do
An arbitrary goal that we take and move
Acting to have as much fun as possible
