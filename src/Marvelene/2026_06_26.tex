\marginnote[1mm]{\textit{This piece is called status symbols.}}


college sweatshirts, degreeless founders, a neat haircut, a full swiss wig,
being recognized as a regular customer, the zip code where you choose to live,

the flat abs of a serious runner, the puffy look of one who never runs,
having a nice car, only ubers, having a foreign accent, or very local ones,

the foods we eat, the foods we'd never touch,
knowing about wine, chocolate, or such and such
having a bodyguard, being on a list, using the latest tech, social media statistics

being too busy to respond quickly to texts, responding to emails instantly,
going to see a movie on opening night, reading the book before the movie,

holding the door open for someone, having the door held open for you,
where we sit at a meeting, the opinions we hold, the knowledge we spew,

having a baby, not having a baby, having a cat, the breed of dog we adopt,
standard dentition, bejewelled diastema, we’ve gotten very good at noticing both.
