\marginnote[1mm]{\textit{Surrender is a skill.}}

I feel most like myself when I am unaware of my image or just don't care,
Do you remember tne evening of us recently in Asaba, singing in the kitchen?
It was packed with about six other women, and I think you were there
As well as Treasure, Cynthia, Joy.\ We were a choir with reckless abandon.
There's a hym we sang `God of mercy and compassion, Look with pity upon me'
I stopped what I was doing and my body started conducting you all with gestures
The music felt so good, I could have wept at that moment, it felt so free
And then we all laughed after, and got back to preparing and serving dishes
