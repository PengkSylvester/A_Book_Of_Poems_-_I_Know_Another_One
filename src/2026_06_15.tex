\hspace{1.18em}A large percentage of human displeasure is in our anticipatory tension
Dreading a future experience such that we feel its potential suffering

We actualize in the now what pains may happen that we're ill-equipped to handle
Anticipatory tension; the illusion of knowledge generating the illusion of control.

My death will eventually come and no one knows if it will be torture or bliss
Yet I grab onto the notion that I deserve more than whatever God wants to bless

Life is happy children laughing and rolling around in the green grass with their family dog
The now is what we are here for.\ And it's destined to go how it goes.\ Don't worry, do observe.
