\marginnote[1mm]{\textit{I'm honoured.} \newthought{- Eme}}

\xout{Hi honoured, I'm in love.}
\resetlinenumber[1]
I'm glad that you Eme (who I've long desired) are now my friend
I yearn to woo you, to boo you, to make you feel lovely to no end.


I plan to write you every morning, or at least every day,
On different topics, whatever musings may come my way to say.

And this is the first of my many letters to solve for our compatibility,
To see if you do not enjoy, \emph{or} \textit{do} love these daily doses of vitamin me.

I must bid you goodnight now so I can rest well for tomorrow.\ Adieu.
Take care of your self, boo, as you know how to do, sweet dreams, God bless you
