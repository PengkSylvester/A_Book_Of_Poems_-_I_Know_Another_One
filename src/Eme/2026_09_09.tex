\marginnote[1mm]{\textit{Building a project in the dark without letting people use it early on is the best way to make something nobody wants.}}


As you go about life you must suffer some pain.

Here's a virtue that averts you from going insane.

Feelings are just things, neither good nor bad for you.

Catch yourself feel the feelings felt true and through.

Throw a tantrum, or respond ``oh look, something new''.

Your body now wants air, food, or a goal it wants to do.

Take some time to breathe or pray on each new feeling each new day.

Patience is a virtue, one not to be thrown away.
