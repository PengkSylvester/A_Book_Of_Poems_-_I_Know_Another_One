\marginnote[1mm]{\textit{I'm so excited to help you win Eme over!} \newthought{- Nma}}

Dear future daughter, before you were born, I had a plan to a better man.
Wouldn't it be nice to have a reminisce on how I wooed your Mum?
It wasn't a case where I smiled, and she just fell apart.
I had to work to win your mother's heart.\ Your mother's smart.
I knew that to engage her I'd have to stand out as the perfect sir;
With the right history, chemistry, stability, and literature.
It wasn't easy, but daddy was a focused man with a plan to boo Eme,
And she let me cook, literally, I cooked for her on our first weekend.
