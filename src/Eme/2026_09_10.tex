\marginnote[1mm]{
    \textit{There is nothing I shall always delight in more than to assist you in unfolding your joys to their highest perfection.} \newthought{- Alexander Hamilton}}


I made you feel uncomfortable\ldots I'm really sorry about that.


I don't know how to give a good first impression at a start.


It would seem I have managed to let you know that I'm not perfect.


No one is, for worth it's worth, perfect to live, laugh, love, or work with.


Uncle Uzor, Obim, and Nonso were all fun to hang out with.


There's a reason I didn't call you a lot while I was around in Fort Worth,


It was to avoid making you feel uncomfortable or pressured to hang out.


Now that I'm away, there can be no suspicion that I'm texting to get in your pants.
