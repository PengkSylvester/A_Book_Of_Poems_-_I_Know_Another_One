Dad embraces who I could be; sees my unfulfilled potential.
Mum embraces who I am; sees her baby, plain and simple.
If I don't listen, Mum frowns.\ If I don't listen, Dad ignores.
I win something and Dad smiles, I lose it and Mum hugs.
It takes a village to raise a child, and it was led by my elders.
They expect to meet grandkids.\ They expect to meet angels.
Are you the rib I was formed from, formed for, or are you a bad thing?
Life is ever a series of partings and meetings.\ And ours is now starting.
