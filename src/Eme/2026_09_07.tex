What is this feeling?\ Is it dopamine or anxiety?

My heart's racing\ldots Why's it happening to me?

Is it because she's here?\ She can affect my tummy?

Blimey\ldots It hurts in a way I can't explain completely.

I barely know her.\ She's a stranger.\ A new memory.

This must be what it feels like to meet someone scary,

Like a celebrity I like, and I also want them to like me.

It's bittersweet to meet a kindred spirit in reality.
