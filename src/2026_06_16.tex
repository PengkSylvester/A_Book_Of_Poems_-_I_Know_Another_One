\begin{fullwidth}
\hspace{1.18em}I can’t defend this intellectually, but it has been true in my experience:
spontaneous compassion is the approach to ethics that feels best and works most
It feels good to be good.\ It feels bad to be mean.\ Something's wrong if it doesn't.
If you want a plan for consistently being good, the dumbest one that is also the most robust
Is to notice that prosocial behaviour is nourishing, and you can do it anonymous
And that peace is only available if you’re on good terms with your conscience
You know that person in your life who made a kind gesture that had important ripple effects?
That interesting stranger whose manner cast a brief spell on you, lingering in your past?
That’s you also, you’re that person.\ Most of your impact on others, you will never guess
\end{fullwidth}
